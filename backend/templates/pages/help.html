🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up the system for the first time? ▼

To set up your Smart Waste Segregator: 1) Create an account on the login page, 2) Connect your ESP32 device to WiFi, 3) Configure the Flask server IP in the ESP32 code, 4) Place sensors in your bins, 5) Start monitoring through the dashboard!

What types of waste should go in wet vs dry bins? ▼

Wet Waste: Food scraps, fruit/vegetable peels, tea bags, coffee grounds, garden waste, cooked food.

Dry Waste: Paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, metal cans, glass, textiles, clean packaging materials.

How accurate are the fill level readings? ▼

Our system uses ultrasonic sensors with ±2% accuracy. Combined with IR and moisture sensors, the system provides highly reliable fill level detection. Readings update every 5 seconds for real-time monitoring.

Can I receive notifications when bins are full? ▼

Yes! Enable browser notifications in Settings. You'll receive alerts when bins reach 80% capacity. You can also configure email/SMS notifications for multiple users.

How do I reset my password? ▼

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and you'll receive a password reset link. Follow the link to create a new password.

What do the environmental impact numbers mean? ▼

Impact metrics show your contribution to sustainability: CO₂ saved (carbon emissions prevented), Trees equivalent (trees that would be needed to offset your waste), Water saved (conservation through recycling), Energy saved (power conserved through proper waste management).

How do I earn points and badges? ▼

Earn points by: Regular bin emptying (10 pts), Maintaining daily streaks (10 pts/day), Proper waste segregation (5 pts), Timely collections before 90% full (20 pts). Badges are unlocked by achieving specific milestones!

Can I manage multiple bins/locations? ▼

Yes! Upgrade to multi-location mode in Settings. You can add unlimited bins across different buildings, floors, or areas. Each location has separate analytics and can be assigned to different users.

What browsers are supported? ▼

The system works on all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions). For best experience, use Chrome or Firefox. Mobile browsers are fully supported. The app is also installable as a PWA.

How do I export my data? ▼

Go to Reports page, select date range and format (PDF/CSV/Excel), then click Generate Report. You can download, print, or email reports directly from the page.
