📖 Waste Segregation Guide

Learn which items go in the wet bin vs dry bin. Proper segregation helps the environment and earns you eco points!

🔍

💧 Wet Waste

Biodegradable / Organic waste

  • 🍌
    Banana Peel
    Fruit peels decompose naturally
  • 🥕
    Vegetable Scraps
    Onion skins, potato peels, etc.
  • 🍛
    Cooked Food
    Leftover rice, curry, bread
  • 🍵
    Tea Bags & Coffee Grounds
    Natural biodegradable waste
  • 🥚
    Egg Shells
    Crushed shells compost well
  • 🌸
    Flowers & Garden Waste
    Leaves, dried flowers, grass
  • 🍖
    Meat & Fish Waste
    Bones, skin, unused parts
  • 🧀
    Spoiled Dairy
    Expired milk, cheese, curd
  • 🍎
    Fruit Peels
    Apple cores, mango peels, orange rinds

♻️ Dry Waste

Recyclable / Non-biodegradable waste

  • 🧴
    Plastic Bottles
    Rinse before disposing
  • 📄
    Paper & Notebooks
    Newspapers, magazines, office paper
  • 📦
    Cardboard
    Flatten boxes before disposing
  • 🥫
    Metal Cans
    Aluminium cans, tin containers
  • 🫙
    Glass Bottles & Jars
    Handle carefully, rinse first
  • 🛍️
    Plastic Bags & Wrappers
    Chip packets, poly bags, wrappers
  • 👕
    Old Clothes & Fabric
    Torn clothes, rags, textile waste
  • 👟
    Rubber & Footwear
    Old slippers, rubber bands
  • 🔌
    E-Waste
    Old cables, broken chargers, small electronics

💡 Pro Tips

🚿

Rinse Containers

Always rinse plastic and glass containers before putting them in the dry bin to prevent contamination.

📦

Separate Packaging

Remove food from packaging. Food goes in wet bin, packaging in dry bin.

🌱

Compost at Home

Kitchen scraps can be composted in a small balcony composter for nutrient-rich soil.

🚫

Reduce Single-Use

Carry reusable bags, bottles, and containers to minimize waste generation.
